# Financial Statement Parser

## Problem:
Analyzing a company's finances typically means hunting through multiple sources, downloading reports, and manually organizing data. It's time-consuming and error-prone.

## Solution:
This tool instantly retrieves and displays a company's key financial statements—including income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow reports—all in one organized, easy-to-read dashboard. Just enter the stock ticker and explore the financial data. You can also download the results as a spreadsheet for further analysis.

## This tool offers:
- **Quick access** to official financial statements for any publicly traded company
- **Clean, organized tables** that are easy to understand
- **Download options** to export data as CSV files for your own analysis
- **All in seconds** instead of hours of manual searching
